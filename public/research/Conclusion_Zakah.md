# Conclusion — The Quranic Meaning of Z-K-W (زكاة)

**Source:** Verse-by-verse study of all **56 distinct Z-K-W root verses** (59 occurrences, 20 word
forms) in the Quran, documented in `3_Study_Notes.md` (56 entries) and synthesized in
`Final_Research_Zakah.md`.

---

## 1. The Root's Core Meaning

The root Z-K-W (ز-ك-و) names **growth-through-purity**: the release of a portion that cleanses what
remains and causes it to increase. Not a percentage, not a receipt, not a once-a-year transaction.

The Quran states each term of the definition:

1. **Purity** — 9:103: "take from their wealth a ṣadaqah by which you **cleanse** them and
   **grow-purify** them."
2. **Growth** — 30:39: ribā "does not grow with God," while the givers of zakāh are named
   **الْمُضْعِفُونَ** (*al-muḍʿifūn*, the multipliers). 2:276: God "makes the charities grow."
3. **The instrument** — 92:18: "who **gives his wealth**, purifying himself."
4. **The act** — 23:4: "**doers** of the zakāh." A tax is paid; a zakāh is done.
5. **The agent** — 4:49, 24:21, 53:32: no one purifies himself; God purifies whom He wills.

---

## 2. The Three Poles

| Pole | Meaning | Lexeme | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STATE** | purity-growth as a quality of a self, soul, child, or food | زَكِيّ · زَكِيَّة · أَزْكَىٰ · زَكَاة | 18:74 · 19:19 · 19:13 · 18:81 · 18:19 · 91:9 |
| **ACT** | the giving that produces it — done, given, sent ahead | الزَّكَاة · إِيتَاء · فَاعِلُونَ | 9:103 · 92:18 · 23:4 · 30:39 · 2:110 |
| **GRANT** | God and His Messenger as the agents who actually purify | يُزَكِّي · يُزَكِّيهِمْ | 4:49 · 24:21 · 2:129 · 3:164 · 62:2 |

The **STATE** pole is what the word *is*. The **ACT** pole is how it is *done*. The **GRANT** pole
is *who makes it work*. The same stem, three directions.

---

## 3. The Two Effects — Cleanse and Grow

The Quran's own gloss-pair is **ṭahāra + zakāh**:

| Verse | Cleansing | Growth |
|---|---|---|
| 9:103 | `tuṭahhiruhum` — cleanses them | `tuzakkīhim` — grows-purifies them |
| 2:232 | `aṭhar` — cleaner | `azkā` — more purifying |
| 33:33 | `yuṭahhirakum` — purify you thoroughly; remove the impurity | the zakāh commanded in the same verse |

`Ṭahāra` is subtraction: remove what defiles. `Zakāh` is addition: cause what remains to grow. The
order is always the same — cleanse, then grow. A reading that makes zakāh only "charity" loses the
growth; a reading that makes it only "growth" loses the cleansing.

---

## 4. The Law of Increase (Ribā vs Zakāh)

| Verse | What grows | What does not |
|---|---|---|
| 30:39 | zakāh given seeking God's face → **the multipliers** | ribā given to increase in people's wealth → **does not grow with God** |
| 2:276 | God **makes the charities grow** | God **erases ribā** |
| 2:277 | the givers: reward, **no fear, no grief** | the accumulator: anxiety |
| 92:18-21 | the giver seeking God's face: **will be satisfied** | the giver seeking repayment: no reward |
| 73:20 | what you send ahead you **find with God** | what is kept is not sent |

The Quran moves the increase-verb from the lender to the giver. Ribā is the attempt to grow by
holding; zakāh is the growth that comes by letting go. This is not a gloss imported from a lexicon:
30:39 says it in the root's own letters.

---

## 5. The Act, Not a Tax

The strongest internal proof is **23:4**: `وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ لِلزَّكَاةِ فَاعِلُونَ` — "those who are
**doers** of the zakāh." Elsewhere the verb is `ātā` (give); here the Quran calls zakāh a **work with
doers**. It sits in a character-list — humility, guarding, chastity, the zakāh, trusts, covenants,
the standing — not in a fiscal list.

The second proof is **92:18**: giving wealth *is* self-purification. The verb's object is the self;
its instrument is money. Money is never the subject of the root.

The third proof is the absence of any rate. The Quran names the act, its effect, its intention, and
its recipients. It gives no percentage. The percentage is a later juristic specification, not a
Quranic datum.

## 6. The Grant — Who Actually Purifies

| Verse | Claim | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| 4:49 | they purify themselves | "rather, God purifies whom He wills" |
| 24:21 | — | "not one of you would ever be pure without God's favour and mercy" |
| 53:32 | — | "do not declare yourselves pure; He knows best who is guarded" |
| 2:174 · 3:77 | they sell revelation for a small price | "He will not purify them" |
| 2:129 · 3:164 · 62:2 | — | the Messenger is sent to **purify** |

The human act is the receptor of a divine grant, never a self-salvation. This is why the Quran can
command the giving and still deny that the giver purifies himself: the command is the channel, not
the cause.

---

## 7. The Breach-Pattern (Sale → Loss)

| Verse | What was sold | What is lost |
|---|---|---|
| 2:174 | concealed the Book, sold it for a small price | no purification; fire in the belly |
| 3:77 | sold God's covenant and oaths for a small price | no share, no speech, no look, no purification |
| 41:7 | withheld the zakāh | reckoned a disbeliever in the Hereafter |
| 4:49 · 53:32 | claimed self-purity | the verdict is corrected |
| 24:21 | followed Satan's steps | purity becomes impossible without grace |
| 2:83 | turned away from the covenant | the giving abandoned with the standing |

The anti-zakāh is always a **sale**: something that should be released is converted into a price.
The withholder does not merely keep money; he sells a covenant, a revelation, or a verdict.

---

## 8. Why It Is Always Paired With the Standing

Zakāh appears in the ṣalāh-pair in 26 of its 59 occurrences. The pairing is not a listing habit; it
is a structure:

| Verse | The pair's work |
|---|---|
| 98:5 | the four acts of the upright religion |
| 2:43 · 2:83 · 5:12 | the Israelite covenant |
| 9:5 · 9:11 | peace and brotherhood conditions |
| 9:18 | maintaining God's houses |
| 22:41 | the first act of the empowered |
| 24:37 | the non-distracted public life |
| 31:4 · 27:3 | the Hereafter-certain |
| 33:33 | the purified household |
| 58:13 | after forgiveness |
| 73:20 | the eased arrangement |

The standing is the vertical act; the zakāh is the horizontal act. The Quran will not let either
stand alone: a standing without giving is incomplete, and a giving without standing is unanchored.
And 87:14-15 puts them in sequence: **purify → mention the Name → stand.**

## 9. The Lexicon

| Lexeme | Form | Meaning | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| ZAKĀH | noun (f.) | the growth-purity; the giving that produces it | 2:43, 2:83, 98:5, etc. (26×) |
| ZAKĀH | indefinite | a purity-endowment; a grade of purity | 19:13 · 18:81 |
| ZAKĀH | indefinite | any act of zakāh-giving | 30:39 |
| ZAKĪ | adjective | pure, guiltless, sound | 18:74 · 19:19 |
| AZKĀ | elative | purer, more wholesome, better | 18:19 · 2:232 · 24:28 · 24:30 |
| ZAKKĀ | Form II verb | to purify and cause to grow | 2:129 · 3:164 · 9:103 · 91:9 |
| TAZAKKĀ | Form V verb | to purify oneself, grow in purity | 87:14 · 20:76 · 35:18 · 92:18 |

The same noun serves the quality (19:13), the act (30:39), and the institution (2:43). The word did
not begin as a tax and acquire a spiritual gloss; it began as a quality of a sound self and became
the name of the act that produces that quality.

---

## 10. The Bridge to Ṣ-L-W

**87:14-15:** "Flourished who purified, and mentioned the Name of his Lord, and stood."
`tazakkā` → `dhakara` → `fa-ṣallā`. The two roots of this project meet in one verse-pair:
purification prepares the standing; the standing carries the purified.

**9:103:** the taken ṣadaqah cleanses and grows them, and the Prophet's standing is tranquility for
them. The giving and the standing are one system.

**92:18-21:** the giver seeks the face of his Lord Most High — the same `wajh Allāh` as 30:39.

---

## 11. What Zakāh Is Not

- **Not merely alms.** Alms are voluntary; zakāh is a covenant clause with a defined act and a
  defined claim on the community (9:11, 9:5, 98:5).
- **Not merely a tax.** A tax is paid; zakāh is *done* (23:4). A tax is collected for a treasury;
  zakāh is given seeking the Face (30:39) and taken for purification (9:103).
- **Not a percentage.** The Quran gives no rate. It gives an intention, a direction, and an effect.
- **Not self-purification.** The claim is forbidden (53:32); the grant is God's (4:49, 24:21).
- **Not a substitute for the standing.** The pair is stable across the whole Quran; neither replaces
  the other.

---

## 12. The Settled Meaning

The root Z-K-W (ز-ك-و) names **growth-through-purity** — the release of a portion that cleanses what
remains and causes it to increase.

- **The STATE pole:** the sound self, the pure child, the wholesome food, the kept boundary, the
  guarded gaze — a quality that can be graded and given.
- **The ACT pole:** the portion taken from wealth, given despite love of it, given without return,
  given seeking the Face — a work with doers, sent ahead, lent to God.
- **The GRANT pole:** God who purifies whom He wills, and the Messenger sent to recite, teach, and
  purify — so that no one claims the verdict for himself.

The root's own law is the law of increase: what is released grows; what is grasped does not. Ribā
is the attempt to grow by holding; zakāh is the growth that comes by letting go. The Quran's word for
the second is the word for purity itself.

The zakāh is both what you **are** (a self being made sound) and what you **do** (a portion released
into the hands of God). Its measure is not a percentage but a direction: **outward, toward the Face,
for the growth of what remains.**
